# How I'd handle a tough developer

**Task:** a short paragraph on how I would handle a situation with a tough developer who is not cooperative.

As a PM I don’t manage developers directly; they usually have their own manager, so my tool is trust, not authority. 
First, I’d talk to the developer one-on-one and ask what’s behind the resistance, then just listen, without defending my position. Often there’s a real reason: unclear requirements, a technical concern, being brought in too late, or too much work. 
If the concern is valid, I take it into account and adjust; when you’re deep in a project it’s easy to miss something important. Then we agree on a concrete next step together, for example his alternative solution or a smaller scope, and I write it down so we both see the same plan. 
If it still doesn’t work, I’d involve his manager to ask for help, not to complain, and I’d tell the developer before I do it. To prevent this in the future, I’d involve developers early, in spec reviews and estimates, so the plan is also theirs.
